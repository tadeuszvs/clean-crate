How to read a result

Limits: this finds the cutoff that MP3 and most AAC encoders leave. High-bitrate AAC, about 256 kbps, sometimes leaves no visible cutoff and can pass. A pass means consistent with lossless. Where the file came from is still the best evidence.

Tempo and key are estimates. Tempo is reported between 88 and 176 BPM, with the half or double reading beside it when that is plausible. Keys show in standard notation (name and key signature) and Camelot. The usual miss is the relative major or minor, which shares the same Camelot number and signature, and still mixes.
